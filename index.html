The Best Movie I've Seen Recently...

The best movie I’ve seen recently was Reel Rock: Riders on the Storm—a 30-minute climbing film featured in the Banff Mountain Film Festival 2026 World Tour.

Riders on the Storm follows Belgian climbers Siebe Vanhee, Nico Favresse, and Sean Villanueva O’Driscoll, along with their filmmaker, Drew Smith, as they attempt to achieve the first free ascent of Patagonia’s Torre Central. They endure 18 days of freezing temperatures and intense storms on the wall, making light of the situation through humour—passing the time by playing music and making popcorn.
